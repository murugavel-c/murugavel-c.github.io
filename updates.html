EU MDR / IVDR

MDCG 2020-16 rev.5 - IVDR classification guidance updated

The MDCG revised its guidance on the IVDR classification rules (Annex VIII) for in-vitro diagnostic devices. The revision clarifies how the IVD rules are applied in practice, which affects the risk class - and therefore the conformity-assessment route - of IVD products.

CDRH town hall on biocompatibility risk assessment

FDA held a town hall discussing ISO 10993-1:2025 and risk-based selection of biocompatibility endpoints for device evaluation - relevant to how manufacturers plan biological evaluation within a risk-management framework.

New 510(k) submission guidances for two dental device types

CDRH issued 510(k) submission guidances covering dental curing lights and dental composite resin devices, describing the performance testing and documentation FDA expects in premarket notifications for these products.

14 Aug 2026
CDSCO

G.S.R. 743(E) - Medical Devices (Second Amendment) Rules, 2026

Devices sterilised at outsourced sites must carry the sterilization site's licence number on the label (after a six-month transition). The amendment also introduces the Ninth Schedule, a fee table for testing and evaluation activities.

14 Aug 2026
CDSCO

G.S.R. 744(E) - Medical Devices (Third Amendment) Rules, 2026

Rule 63 is amended to include European Union countries after Japan in the list of accepted reference markets, and QMS provisions for Class A non-sterile, non-measuring (NSNM) devices are added in Rules 19H and 19J.
